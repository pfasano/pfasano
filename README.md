# Peter Fasano

**Founder & CEO, MassLogic Labs**

I founded MassLogic Labs and built MIMO™, the brand intelligence platform for the enterprise. Brand Intelligence that Governs Your AI.

## What we're building

It's been my experience that after decades of consulting, developing strategies, implementing policy playbooks, training, and measuring the results with my clients and in-house, I saw the same pattern over and over again. There was little compliance, short attention spans, and the materials quickly became shelfware on a drive, in an inbox, in a webinar that went unused.

In the era of generative AI, I saw an opportunity to turn these undervalued assets into actual knowledge for AI, for the teams, and now for their agentic marketing systems.

MIMO captures all of the human intelligence: the procedures, experience, and intuition of the marketers within a brand, the agencies that support them, and the measured results of putting the brand into market. Customer context stays in the customer's tenant.

## Platform

- **MIMO Intelligence**: the governed context and memory layer.
- **MIMO Orchestration**: agent and marketing technology orchestration.
- **MIMO Workspace**: the command surface a customer deploys, including MIMO Canvas and Brand Radar™.
- **MIMO Marketspace**: a curated marketplace for licensed IP and certified agents.

Grounded in the MASB Universal Marketing Dictionary. MASB is our inaugural standards partner.

## Stack & approach

Agent orchestration with governed, on-record execution. MIMO MCP connector and Skill live. A2A interoperability and graph memory under active design.
